## 3. Red Hat Certification Pathways: RHCSA and RHCE

Red Hat offers professional certifications for Linux administration and automation. These are **separate** from the free RH024 introductory course.

| Certification | Exam | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) | EX200 | Core Red Hat Enterprise Linux system administration |
| Red Hat Certified Engineer (RHCE) | EX294 | Automation of Linux administration tasks using Ansible |

### Example learning progression

1. **RH024** — Start with the free Linux technical overview.
2. **RH124** — Build foundational system administration skills.
3. **RH134** — Continue with more advanced administration topics.
4. **EX200 / RHCSA** — Prepare for and take the RHCSA certification exam.
5. **RH294** — Learn Linux automation with Ansible.
6. **EX294 / RHCE** — Prepare for and take the RHCE certification exam.

> [!NOTE]
> The courses and certification exams in this pathway may require payment. The free RH024 course does not cover their fees.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Red Hat Linux training completely free?**
Some introductory learning is free. RH024 is identified as free, on-demand training. Other Red Hat courses, subscriptions and exams may require payment.

**Can I get RHCSA for free by completing RH024?**
No. RH024 does not include the RHCSA (EX200) exam. You must separately meet the exam requirements and pay any applicable fees.

**Is RHCE included in the 50% discount?**
The 50% offer discussed here is for the eligible OpenShift AI learning subscription offer. It should not be interpreted as a discount on every Red Hat course or certification.

**Is the OpenShift AI certification free?**
No. The offer is a 50% discount on an eligible paid learning subscription. The offer information identifies an EX267 exam attempt as included, subject to the official subscription and promotion terms.

**When does the 50% discount end?**
The advertised promotion ends on 31 December 2026. The stated promotion period is 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2026.

**Is there a deadline for RH024?**
No general enrolment deadline is stated for RH024 in the opportunity information. Check the official course page for current availability.
